Our Team & Contributors

EagleReach is driven by a shared belief that technology can make a meaningful difference in people's lives. Alongside its technical development, the platform welcomes ideas that help address real community needs.

Keerthi Peddireddy

Community Welfare & Public Services Contributor

Keerthi Peddireddy has contributed an important idea to EagleReach's evolving community welfare vision: helping individuals and families discover healthcare benefits, government assistance programs, and essential public services available in their local area.

Her suggestion focuses on making community resources easier to discover through location-based information, allowing residents to explore relevant services using their ZIP code or locality.

This contribution reflects EagleReach's broader mission to go beyond civic information and explore practical ways technology can support public awareness, community well-being, and access to essential services.
