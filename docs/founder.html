Founder

Vikesh Bairam

Vikesh Bairam is a technology professional with over seven years of cross-functional experience across engineering, data analytics, DevOps, and enterprise systems.

He currently works as a Test Engineer at Jabil in Florence, Kentucky, where he focuses on system reliability, quality engineering, and operational improvements.

Previously, Vikesh worked as an Account Manager and DevOps Engineer at Surfacs by Pacific in Cincinnati, Ohio, where he helped bridge the gap between business needs and technical solutions.

Earlier in his career, he worked as a Data Engineer at Techtatte Systems and as a Senior Data Analyst at S&P Global, where he developed data-driven insights for enterprise decision making.

He also gained international experience as a Senior Supply Chain Specialist at Auscomp Computers in Australia.

Education

  • Master of Science in Information Systems & Management University of Illinois Springfield
  • Bachelor of Computers and Commerce University of Allahabad
  • Bachelor of Psychology and Arts Osmania University

Interests

Outside of work, Vikesh enjoys running, pickleball, table tennis, and cricket. He believes that staying active and engaged with the community builds stronger connections and a healthier lifestyle.

Through EagleReach, Vikesh aims to promote civic awareness and make government information more accessible to citizens.
