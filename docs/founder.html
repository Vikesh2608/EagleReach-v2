Founder

Vikesh Bairam

Vikesh Bairam is the Founder and Full Stack Software Engineer behind EagleReach, a civic intelligence platform designed to enhance how citizens access and interact with public information. He holds a Master’s degree in Information Systems from the University of Illinois Springfield and currently works as a Data Analyst and Test Engineer at Jabil.

With a strong foundation in software engineering, data analytics, and system design, Vikesh has developed EagleReach as a complete end-to-end solution—from concept and architecture to deployment and production. The platform demonstrates his ability to build scalable, user-centric systems that integrate real-time data and public APIs into a seamless digital experience.

EagleReach was created to address the fragmentation of civic information across multiple sources. By leveraging geospatial intelligence, API integrations, and modern web technologies, Vikesh engineered a unified platform that delivers location-based insights on representatives, news, weather, and essential services.

In addition to full stack development, Vikesh has implemented DevOps practices to ensure reliability and continuous delivery. He designed and deployed the application using cloud infrastructure on Render, establishing a CI/CD workflow that enables automated builds, seamless updates, and efficient version control. This end-to-end ownership highlights his capability to manage the complete software lifecycle from development to production.

His experience working in data-driven environments has influenced the performance, scalability, and architecture of EagleReach. By combining engineering expertise with a strong understanding of data systems, he has built a platform that is both technically robust and socially impactful.

Vikesh continues to expand EagleReach with a long-term vision of evolving it into a comprehensive civic intelligence ecosystem, incorporating advanced technologies such as artificial intelligence, predictive analytics, and real-time decision support systems to further strengthen civic engagement at scale.
