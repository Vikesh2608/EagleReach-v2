Founder

Vikesh Bairam is a technology professional with over 7 years of experience in data analytics, software testing, and engineering across global organizations.

He holds a Master’s degree in Information Systems from the University of Illinois Springfield and currently works at Jabil, where he contributes to data-driven decision making and engineering solutions.

Vikesh built EagleReach as a civic intelligence platform to bridge the gap between citizens and accessible public information. The goal is to empower individuals with real-time insights about their representatives, local communities, news, weather, and emergency resources — all in one place using just a ZIP code.

His long-term vision is to expand EagleReach into a nationwide civic engagement platform that supports transparency, accessibility, and informed decision-making.
