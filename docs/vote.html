How to Vote

Registration deadlines, polling hours, ID rules and mail voting are set by each state. Use the official sources below to check the rules where you vote.

📝 Register or check deadlines ↗vote.gov — official U.S. government voter registration site 📍 Find your polling place ↗Can I Vote — links to every state's election office 🗳️ Voting and elections guide ↗USA.gov — absentee, early voting and voter ID basics ♿ Accessible voting & help ↗U.S. Election Assistance Commission voter resources

U.S. federal general elections are held on the Tuesday after the first Monday in November. EagleReach is nonpartisan and does not endorse candidates or parties.
