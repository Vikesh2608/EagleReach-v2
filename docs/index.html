About EagleReach

EagleReach is a civic intelligence platform built to help citizens easily access information about their local government, public representatives, news, weather, and emergency services. Our mission is to strengthen civic engagement and transparency by providing a platform where citizens can stay informed and connected to the leaders who represent them. EagleReach empowers communities by making public information accessible, reliable, and easy to understand.

Founder

EagleReach was founded by Vikesh with the goal of building a civic technology platform that helps citizens understand their government and participate in the democratic process. The vision is to improve civic awareness, strengthen transparency, and create a platform where communities can access important public information quickly and easily.
