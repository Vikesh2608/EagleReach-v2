About EagleReach

EagleReach is a civic intelligence platform designed to help citizens access information about their local government, representatives, community issues, news, and emergency resources. Our mission is to strengthen civic awareness and participation by making government information easier to access for every community.

Founder

EagleReach was founded by Vikesh with the vision of improving civic transparency and engagement. The platform connects citizens with government information, public representatives, and community issues.
