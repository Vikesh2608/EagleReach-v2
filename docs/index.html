About EagleReach

EagleReach is a public, open-source civic assistant for U.S. residents. One ZIP code unlocks what people need right now — your elected representatives with official links and phone numbers, voter information and education, election dates, weather, city updates, traffic, news, and the bills moving through Congress.

Why this exists. Democracy works best when access is simple. EagleReach brings civic info together — cleanly, quickly, and without tracking.

  • 100% open data, no sign-in, no paywalls, no tracking.
  • Frictionless: one place, one search, human-readable results.
  • Trustworthy sources: GovTrack, vote.gov, Open-Meteo, city open data.
  • Accessible: large touch targets, keyboard friendly, dark mode, mobile-ready.
  • Extensible: easy to add cities, states, and services.

Vision. Make civic participation as easy as checking the weather.
