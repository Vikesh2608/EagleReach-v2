About EagleReach

EagleReach is a civic intelligence platform built to help citizens quickly access information about their government, public representatives, local news, weather, and emergency services. The mission of EagleReach is to strengthen civic engagement by providing transparent access to public information and connecting communities to the leaders who represent them.

Founder

EagleReach was founded by Vikesh with the goal of building a platform that simplifies civic awareness and encourages citizen participation in government processes. The vision is to empower communities with reliable civic data and tools that improve transparency and access to public leadership.
